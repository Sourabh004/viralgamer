01 // Who we are

Powering India's esports culture

Viral Gamer is an Indian esports media and PR organization powered by 1.3M+ followers across social platforms and a 20M+ monthly reach through multiple community pages.

We specialize in brand integrations, promotions, social media management and on-ground event coverage, while delivering exclusive esports content including live tournament updates, interviews, podcasts and more.

Our mission is to connect brands with the rapidly growing gaming community and create impactful experiences in the Indian esports ecosystem.

Our vision

To be India's leading esports media platform, empowering gamers and brands while seamlessly connecting audiences through impactful content and meaningful engagement.
